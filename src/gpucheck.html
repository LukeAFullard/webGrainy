GPU check

This page checks whether your computer's graphics card (via WebGPU) gives the same results as the CPU when webGrainy analyses grains. Some GPUs compute in reduced precision (float16), which could change results. It runs five short test images (including a photo with a large black area) on the GPU and then on the CPU, and compares them. Nothing is uploaded: everything runs in your browser.

Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. The first run downloads the model (Cellpose-SAM 183–617 MB depending on the weights, or U-Net 26 MB), which is cached for the app afterwards. The CPU part of the Cellpose-SAM check can take several minutes.
